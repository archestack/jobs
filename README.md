# Archestack jobs

Open roles at the teams that build the open-source AI projects on [archestack.com](https://archestack.com/), and a form for people who contribute to those projects and are open to work.

- **Teams hiring:** https://archestack.com/jobs/ lists each team next to the projects it builds, with roles copied from the team's own careers page twice a day.
- **Open to work:** [fill in the form](https://github.com/archestack/jobs/issues/new?template=open-to-work.yml). You need one pull request merged in the last 24 months into a project on our lists. Free, shown for 90 days, and you leave by closing your issue.

Questions or a removal request: hello@archestack.com.
